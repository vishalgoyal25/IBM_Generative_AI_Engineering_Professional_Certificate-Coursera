   └── Decoder Only ──► GPT

How GPT Fits

Input Tokens

 ↓
Masked Decoder Stack
 ↓
Linear
 ↓
Softmax
 ↓
Next Token

No Encoder.

Ultimate One-Line Summary
Transformer = Encoder + Decoder

BERT = Encoder Only

GPT = Decoder Only

This is the single most important architectural relationship to remember before entering Courses 13–16 (Fine-Tuning, LLMs, RAG, and Agentic AI).
